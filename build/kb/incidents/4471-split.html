After the engineering update

Engineering closed #4471 on 5 October. The parser problem is fixed, and its ticket is closed as completed. The hint problem is closed as won’t fix because the booking-v3 form is retired. The parser fix did not cover the hint problem. The one-day-move ticket stays open because the update does not mention it. Its cause is not confirmed.

parser

The reporter sees a booking with the day and the month swapped. The report comes from a tenant whose locale writes the day first, through the app, email, or a form that hints the day first. The cause was that the shared parser read every date month first. Engineering fixed the parser to read day-first dates for EU locales.

hint

The reporter sees a swapped date on a booking made through the booking-v3 form. The report comes through the form channel from a day-first tenant that runs booking-v3. The cause is known: the form prints an MM/DD/YYYY hint, people type the month first, and the form reads the day first. The parser fix did not change this. The booking-v3 form is retired, so engineering will not fix the hint.

one-day-move

The reporter sees a date that moves by one day. The month stays the same. The reports mention two cities, say the problem happens only in June, or describe an invite that arrives before the booking. These signs point to a time zone problem. The cause is not confirmed.

How the count works

A report that appears twice in the export has one id, and we count it once. The cause comes from the tenant’s locale and booking form. The intake tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser, and we do not use it. The one-day-move reports are the desk’s own list in desk-notes.json.
